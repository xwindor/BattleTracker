# The player rolls their lost dice on a GM-side jack-out or deck removal

Status: needs-triage
From: the Matrix rulings review, 2026-09-28

## What's wanted

When the GM jacks a player's decker out, or removes their deck, the dice the
character loses are resolved on the GM's side at once today. Wanted (decision
0012, "a player always rolls the Initiative dice they lose"): the player gets
the prompt to roll their lost dice; it waits for them through a reconnect;
and the GM has a "roll for them" button for an absent player.
