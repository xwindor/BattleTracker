# Review the old rulings: initiative, Seize, astral and log

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01

## Question

Which of the 12 old rulings in Session A of the inventory
([old-rulings.md](../research/old-rulings.md), A1–A12) does Xavier re-confirm
for the fresh rulings list?

Each kept ruling is re-checked against the core rulebook through the rules
look-up, with the page it fills a gap next to, and reworded in plain language.
Entries the inventory marks "App behaviour" go to the product decisions
instead of the rulings list. Printed rules can be dropped, since the look-up
will find them again, unless Xavier wants them listed.

## Answer

Decided with Xavier, 2026-09-28. Page checks were done by reading the core
rulebook pages directly (the rules look-up helper could not be started that
day); each is quoted below so it can be re-checked.

**Standing rule for all the rulings reviews:** the rulings list holds table
calls only. Rules the book states outright are left off; the rules look-up
finds them when needed.

**Carry forward to the rulings list (7):**

1. **No floor on Initiative Score.** It can go negative and is never stopped
   at 0. The book never states a floor; its own example reaches –4 (Core
   p. 160), and Interrupts can "reduce their Initiative Score below 0" (Core
   p. 167).
2. **No roll, no turn.** Someone in the fight who hasn't rolled this Combat
   Turn is skipped, can't take Initiative-costing Interrupts, and doesn't keep
   the Combat Turn going. The book only covers joiners who roll at once (Core
   p. 160).
3. **Seize needs a roll first.** The control is refused, not warned. The book
   moves a seizer to the top "regardless of your Initiative Score" and orders
   several seizers "in order of their Initiative Scores" (Core p. 160–161).
4. **Seize any time after rolling.** It can be used outside your own turn,
   costs 1 Edge, never costs Initiative, and isn't an Interrupt Action. The
   book gives it no timing (Core p. 160), unlike Delaying (Core p. 161).
5. **Lieutenant goes first on a tie with his team, even past a third party.**
   The book says "they always go first" against their own team (Core p. 381).
   A three-way tie makes the two tie rules go round in a circle; the lieutenant
   wins.
6. **Astral projection adds two Initiative Dice on top of current dice** (3D6
   for someone with no bonus dice); returning removes them. The book says 2D6
   at Core p. 101 and p. 159, "gains the die" at Core p. 160, and "+2D6 (3D6
   total)" at Core p. 314. Adding on top follows "rolls the extra Initiative
   Dice and adds the sum" (Core p. 160).
7. **GM and NPC rolls are visible to players by default.** The GM can hide
   the next roll or all GM rolls. The book leaves this to the group (Core
   p. 330).

**Carry forward as a product decision (1):** a Condition Monitor's maximum
never appears in any log, only the damage done, so players earn "how many hits
until it drops" by fighting.

**Dropped (4):**
- Two are printed rules: the 0-or-below limit (Core p. 160), and Seize
  changing rank only (Core p. 161).
- One is superseded: the old astral wording, now merged into item 6.
- One only fixed a bug: announcing a new combatant when they first roll.
