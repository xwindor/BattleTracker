# Review the old rulings: grunts and NPC groups

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01

## Question

Which of the 11 old rulings in Session B of the inventory
([old-rulings.md](../research/old-rulings.md), B1–B11) does Xavier re-confirm
for the fresh rulings list? This includes settling B7 (final Physical damage
exactly equal to Body), which was never decided.

Same method as the initiative review: re-check each kept ruling against the
core rulebook, write the page down, and reword it plainly.

## Answer

Decided with Xavier, 2026-09-28. The rules look-up helper couldn't be
started, so the pages were read directly (Core p. 379–381); the quotes below
are from those pages.

**Carry forward to the rulings list (4):**

1. **A wound on any grunt slows the whole group.** It's deliberately against
   the book, which says injury modifiers "might cause some of the grunts to
   act on a different Initiative Score than the rest of their team" (Core
   p. 379). How it works:
   - The group's shared score drops when a member takes a wound.
   - Joining or leaving the group never moves it.
   - A downed member's wound still counts.
   - Healing gives it back.
   - A grunt who needs his own score is split onto his own row.
2. **Grunts have no Edge attribute, so they count as 0 in tie-breaks.** Their
   Group Edge pool (equal to Professional Rating) is separate and isn't used
   for ties. The book: grunts "don't have their own Edge attributes at all"
   (Core p. 380); tie-breaks use Edge (Core p. 159). This replaces the old
   "the book contradicts itself" wording, which the page doesn't bear out.
3. **Final Physical damage exactly equal to Body: the grunt is alive.** The
   book: alive if "less than the grunt's Body", dead if "greater than" (Core
   p. 379). It's silent on equal. New decision; the app currently shows
   "undetermined".
4. **Healing a downed grunt below full brings him back into the fight.** The
   book's "out of action for the rest of the fight" (Core p. 379) is read as
   "while the monitor stays full", because healing is the only way to correct
   a mis-typed killing blow.

**Carry forward as a product decision (1):** editing a stat is a correction,
not an in-game event. A monitor resizes when Body or Willpower is edited,
recorded damage is kept (cut off at the new size), and a downed character
whose monitor grows stands back up. This applies to every participant.

**Dropped (6):**
- Printed rules, or direct consequences of one: the split-off grunt keeps his
  single monitor (Core p. 379); a merged group rolls fresh with the
  late-entry penalty (Core p. 160); a killing blow can exceed the boxes left
  (Core p. 379).
- App behaviour: merging grunts who are all down; the group flagged when its
  last member goes down; an emptied group left as a plain row.

**Follow-up for the switch:** ruling 3 changes what the app does. Today it
shows "undetermined". This needs a to-do item.
