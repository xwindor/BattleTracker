## Answer

Decided with Xavier, 2026-09-28. The rules look-up helper couldn't be
started. Pages were read directly, except where marked "from the notebook"
(the kept rules notebook, entries checked twice on 2026-09-11).

**Carry forward to the rulings list (10):**

1. **IC Initiative = Host Data Processing + Host Rating + 4D6.** The book
   gives IC "its own… Initiative Score", 4D6, and "IC uses the Matrix
   attributes of its host", but never the attribute (Core p. 247).
2. **IC's Condition Monitor = 8 + half the Host Rating, rounded up.** The size
   is never given (Core p. 247); devices are 8 + half Device Rating (Core
   p. 228, from the notebook). The old "p. 48" citation is dropped.
3. **IC launched at the start of a Combat Turn rolls normally and acts that
   turn, with no late-entry penalty.** The host launches IC "at the beginning
   of each Combat Turn" (Core p. 247).
4. **Tied IC with nothing to compare act simultaneously.** This is the book's
   GM option (Core p. 159).
5. **A decker in VR has one row only, never a second row for the body.** The
   body can't act; if attacked, the GM treats it as unaware (Core p. 189).
   The book: "your body goes limp" (Core p. 229). A new Initiative type
   replaces the old one (Core p. 160).
6. **Cold-sim is exactly 3D6, hot-sim exactly 4D6.** Augmentations don't add
   on top, and returning to AR restores the previous dice. The book pulls both
   ways: "Base" dice on the chart (Core p. 159), but "+3D6" and the 5D6 cap
   (Core p. 229–230). **This deliberately differs from astral**, which adds
   on top. Record this so nobody "fixes" one to match the other.
7. **A host never gets a row in the order.** It perceives through its IC,
   which "instantly share[s] spotting information" (Core p. 247).
8. **A mark on a slaved device also lands on its master and counts toward the
   master's three** (Core p. 233; the book never says whether it counts toward
   the cap).
9. **No cooldown after a reboot or jack-out.** The reset itself is printed
   (Core p. 242); the book gives no cooldown.
10. **Files live only on commlinks, decks and hosts.** Changed by Xavier from
    the old "any device" ruling: files on a weapon make no sense. The book only
    distinguishes "in a host" from "not" (Core p. 239, from the notebook), so
    this is a table call.

**Carry forward as product decisions (4):**
- **Marks passed up automatically** are shown before you commit and are never
  removed automatically.
- **The app never fills in a made-up number.** Blank beats a plausible wrong
  value (e.g. statblock Data Processing).
- **The PAN slave cap warns, never blocks.**
- **A player always rolls the Initiative dice they lose,** including when the
  GM jacks them out or removes their deck. The prompt waits for them through a
  reconnect, and the GM can press "roll for them". Changed by Xavier from "GM
  rolls on a GM-side jack-out".

**Folded in:** "The Matrix side records numbers and never applies effects"
becomes the Matrix example under "tracker, not a referee".

**Deferred to the extra product decisions review:** "announce before moving an
icon's children" (the "tell before changing data" principle).
