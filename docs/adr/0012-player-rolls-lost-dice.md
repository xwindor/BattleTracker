# A player always rolls the Initiative dice they lose

Decided: 2026-09-28.

When a player's character loses Initiative Dice mid-turn, the player rolls
the lost dice themselves. This includes when the GM jacks them out or removes
their deck. The roll prompt waits for them through a reconnect, and the GM can
press "roll for them" if the player is absent.

This was changed on 2026-09-28 from "the GM's side resolves a GM-side jack-out
at once". A to-do item covers building it.
