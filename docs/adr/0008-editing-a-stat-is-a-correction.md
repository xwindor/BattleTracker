# Editing a stat is a correction, not an in-game event

Decided: 2026-09-28.

When the GM edits a stat, the app treats it as fixing a typing mistake, not
as something that happened in the fight. For example, when Body or Willpower
is edited:
- the Condition Monitor resizes;
- damage already recorded is kept, cut off at the new size;
- a downed character whose monitor grows stands back up.

This applies to every participant.
