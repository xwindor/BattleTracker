# A tracker, not a referee

Decided: 2026-09-27, re-confirmed 2026-09-28.

The app remembers and shows what's happening at the table. It never decides
an outcome the GM would normally decide: no resolving tests, no deciding
success or failure. The GM decides; the app records.

Examples:
- **The Matrix side records numbers and never applies effects.** It keeps the
  numbers the GM gives it (damage, marks, Overwatch Score) and never changes
  attributes or dice pools on its own because of them.
- **Matrix noise is a reminder only.** The GM sets it and the app shows it, but
  it is never taken off any dice pool.
- **Modelling a subsystem's whole decision tree is out of scope,** for example
  every Matrix action's test and its consequences.
