# Player screens can be locked, with guarantees

Decided: 2026-09-27, re-confirmed 2026-09-28.

A player's screen may be blocked until they act (for example, rolling
Initiative), but only when the GM asked for it. The guarantees:
- The prompt reappears by itself after a reconnect, a refresh or a late join.
- Every way a player can stop owing the action also closes the prompt, so
  nobody is left stranded behind it.

This applies to any future must-answer prompt, not just the Initiative roll.
