# Rules must be right wherever the app does the maths

Decided: 2026-09-28.

Wherever the app calculates something itself, it must follow the rules
exactly: Initiative Score changes, wound modifiers, mark caps, IC Initiative
and the like. How tests are resolved isn't the app's job (see
[tracker, not a referee](0001-tracker-not-referee.md)), and finding a rule in
the book doesn't mean building it.
