# SR5E Battle Tracker

A GM's live initiative and combat tracker for Shadowrun 5th Edition, with a
player view kept in sync. This glossary fixes the words used for the parts of
the app so everyone means the same thing.

## Language

### Who is in the fight

**Participant**:
Anyone with a place in the initiative list: a character, a grunt, or a grunt group.
_Avoid_: Character (when a group is included), actor, combatant

### The GM screen

**Top bar**:
The strip across the top of the GM screen holding round status, the round buttons and the room controls.
_Avoid_: Toolbar, header

**Warning area**:
The always-visible lines under the top bar that say why someone can't act yet or what is still owed.
_Avoid_: Notices, alerts, banners

**Rolls-owed bar**:
The bar that appears whenever any participant still owes an Initiative roll, whether between Combat Turns or mid-turn.
_Avoid_: Prep card, Pending Rolls card, Initiative Prep

**Initiative list**:
The always-visible list of participants in acting order, carrying the controls used every turn.
_Avoid_: Tracker, combat list, participant list

**Row menu**:
The ⋯ menu on a participant's row, holding occasional actions on that participant.
_Avoid_: More menu, overflow menu, trailing buttons

**Participant panel**:
The right-hand panel showing one participant's details (Condition, Stats, Deck, Magic or Group); it opens only when asked for and stays on that participant until changed.
_Avoid_: Character panel, details bar, side panel, drawer

**Matrix panel**:
The wide right-hand panel for running the Matrix, sharing one slot with the participant panel.
_Avoid_: Matrix module, Matrix run panel

**Bottom strip**:
The always-visible action log and dice roller along the bottom of the GM screen, collapsible to one line.
_Avoid_: Footer, log panel
